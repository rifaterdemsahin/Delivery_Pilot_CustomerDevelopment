## 🚨 Critical Flags & Gaps

### **1. The Premise Needs Stress-Testing**

Your entire funnel hinges on “AI Engineer is THE role” being accepted as truth. But:

- **Counterargument risk**: Many enterprises are creating *specialized* AI roles (ML Ops, Prompt Engineers, AI Product Managers), not consolidating into one
- **Recommendation**: You need data/case studies proving consolidation is happening. Without this, sophisticated buyers will push back hard on sales calls

**Action**: Add a “proof deck” to your webinar showing:

- LinkedIn job posting trends (consolidated roles increasing)
- Quotes from CTOs/engineering leaders
- Salary data showing “AI Engineer” generalists out-earning specialists

### **2. “Daily Coaching” May Not Scale**

If you hit your goal of 100 sales/year ($1M), that’s 100 students needing daily attention.

- **Math check**: Even 15 min/day per student = 25 hours/day of coaching
- **Recommendation**: Clarify what “daily coaching” actually means. Is it:
  - Group office hours (scalable)?
  - Async feedback on projects (scalable)?
  - 1-on-1 calls (not scalable)?

**Action**: Define your coaching model more precisely. Consider cohort-based delivery (30-50 students every 6 weeks) rather than rolling enrollment.

### **3. The “Agentic Framework” Needs More Clarity**

Right now it’s a buzzword in your plan. For this to work as your differentiator:

- It needs to be **teachable but proprietary** (like YC’s “Ramen Profitability” or Gartner’s “Hype Cycle”)
- Buyers need to understand *what it is* before the sales call

**Action**: Create a free “Agentic Framework” mini-course or white paper. Make it substantial enough to establish authority, vague enough to require your program for mastery.

### **4. Conversion Math Seems Optimistic**

Your KPIs assume:

- 25,000 emails → 7,500 webinar registrants (30%)
- 3,000 webinar attendees (40% show rate)
- 600 applications (20%)
- 420 qualified calls (70%)
- **105-140 sales (25-33% close rate)**

**Reality check**:

- 25-33% close rate on $10K B2C is *possible* but requires world-class sales skills
- Most B2C programs at this price point see 10-20% close rates

**Action**: Build your financial model with 15% close rate as baseline, 25% as stretch goal. You may need 50K+ emails to hit $1M.

### **5. Missing: Student Outcomes & Risk Reversal**

You mention “90%+ success rate” but don’t define success or address refund/guarantee policy.

**Action**:

- Define success metric clearly (e.g., “90% of graduates ship a production AI agent within 90 days”)
- Consider a guarantee: “If you don’t [achieve X outcome] after completing the program, we’ll work with you for free until you do”

### **6. Competition & Differentiation Gap**

This plan doesn’t address:

- How you differ from Deeplearning.ai, fast.ai, Weights & Biases courses
- How you differ from AI Engineering bootcamps emerging now
- Why someone should pay $10K vs. self-learning + ChatGPT

**Action**: Add a competitive analysis slide to your webinar. Position against “cheap courses” (no accountability), bootcamps (no philosophy), and self-learning (no structure).

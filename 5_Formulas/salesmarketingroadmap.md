Here is a comprehensive sales and marketing roadmap for your "Delivery Pilot" AI Engineer program, built on your core premise that the "AI Engineer" will be the consolidated, essential role in the future enterprise.
This plan focuses on a high-ticket B2C (Business-to-Consumer) model, using your free videos to attract individuals and convert them into your $10,000 "AI Engineer Accelerator" program.
Executive Summary: The "Delivery Pilot" Roadmap
 * Product: The ~$10,000 "AI Engineer Accelerator" Program.
 * Core Promise: We transform ambitious professionals into "AI Engineers" – the only role that will matter in the new enterprise. We take you from "the Unknown" and, using our proprietary "Agentic Framework," assessment platform, and daily coaching, mold you into a high-impact, future-proof professional.
 * Core Message (The "Idea"): "The Great Consolidation is here. Post-2020, AI has made specialized tech roles obsolete. Enterprises no longer want a data scientist, a backend dev, and an ML engineer. They want one person who can do it all: The AI Engineer. We are the only ones who train this new role."
 * Target Audience (B2C): "The Ambitious Professional." This is a 25-40 year old software engineer, data analyst, project manager, or even a tech-savvy business leader who sees the rise of AI and is worried about their career becoming commoditized. They are smart, driven, and willing to invest in themselves to gain a decisive edge.
Phase 1: Awareness & Authority (Top of Funnel)
Goal: To establish your premise as the undeniable truth and build a massive audience of your target persona. Your free videos are the engine for this.
Core Tactic: The "Provocative Educator" Content Strategy
Your free videos should not be generic "Learn Python" tutorials. They must sell your philosophy.
 * Platforms:
   * YouTube (Primary): The hub for your long-form "Stairway to AI Engineer" video series.
   * LinkedIn (Secondary): Repurpose video clips and write text posts about your "Great Consolidation" premise. Excellent for B2B visibility.
   * X (Twitter) & TikTok/Reels: Short, sharp, provocative clips and threads to drive traffic to YouTube.
 * Content Pillars (Video Series Ideas):
   * The "Big Idea" (Your Premise):
     * "Why 'Software Engineer' is a Dead-End Job"
     * "The Great Consolidation: Why 90% of Tech Roles Will Vanish"
     * "The 2020 Skill-Shift: What Your Boss Expects vs. What You Can Do"
     * "The Only Tech Role That Matters in 5 Years: The AI Engineer"
   * The "Agentic Framework" (Your Solution):
     * "Stop Learning Tools, Start 'Thinking Agentically'"
     * "How I Built an AI Agent to Do My Old Job"
     * "The 'Stairway' Framework: From 'Unknown' to AI Problem-Solver"
   * Practical Philosophy (The "How"):
     * "A Day in the Life of a Real AI Engineer (Not a 'Prompt Engineer')"
     * "I Gave 3 Developers an AI Problem. Only One Succeeded. Here's Why."
     * "Project Breakdown: Building a Full-Stack AI Application"
 * Lead Magnet (The "Hook"):
   * Don't just ask people to subscribe. At the end of every video, drive them to a specific lead magnet.
   * Good: "Download my 5-Point 'Agentic Framework' Checklist."
   * Better: "Take our free 10-minute 'AI Engineer Readiness Assessment.' See how your skills stack up against the new enterprise standard."
   * This assessment perfectly tees up your paid assessment platform. It shows them their gaps and gets their email.
Phase 2: Interest & Nurturing (Middle of Funnel)
Goal: To convert your email list from passive viewers into qualified leads who believe in your solution and are ready to invest.
 * Tactic 1: The "Insider" Email Nurture Sequence
   * Once they download the assessment/checklist, they enter this automated sequence.
   * Email 1 (The Welcome): "You've seen the future. Here's the map. (And your assessment results)."
   * Email 2 (The Problem): "The 'skill gap' isn't what you think. It's a thinking gap. Here's what the enterprise really wants."
   * Email 3 (The Solution): "Introducing the 'Stairway' and our 'Agentic Framework.' This is how we bridge the gap. (Testimonial/Case Study snippet)."
   * Email 4 (The Bridge): "I'm hosting a private, live workshop: 'The AI Engineer Roadmap: From Obsolete to Indispensable in 90 Days.'"
 * Tactic 2: The Live Webinar (The "Bridge to Sale")
   * This is your most important conversion event. It's a 60-90 minute live presentation.
   * Structure:
     * The Premise (30 min): Deep dive into "The Great Consolidation." Show data, quotes, and examples of how AI is increasing expectations and eliminating roles. Agitate the pain.
     * The Path (15 min): Introduce your "Agentic Framework" and "Stairway" as the only logical solution.
     * The Product (15 min): "This is why I built the AI Engineer Accelerator."
       * Show the Assessment Platform (the "before").
       * Explain the Daily Coaching (the "process").
       * Show the "Agentic Framework" curriculum (the "map").
       * Show the "after" (case studies, project examples).
     * The Offer (CTA): "This is a $10,000 program. It is not for everyone. It's an intensive, high-touch accelerator. If you are serious about this transformation, I invite you to book a 1-on-1 'AI Career Clarity Call' with my team."
Phase 3: Decision & Conversion (Bottom of Funnel)
Goal: To convert qualified, high-intent leads into $10,000 customers using a high-touch sales process.
 * The Funnel:
   * Webinar CTA -> Application Form: The "Book a Call" link does not go to a calendar. It goes to a strict application form.
     * Questions: "What is your current role?" "What is your income?" "Why do you need this now?" "On a scale of 1-10, how serious are you about investing $10k in your
       career?" (Must select 8-10 to proceed).
   * Application Review -> Booking Link: You manually review applications. Only qualified people are sent a link to book their 1-on-1 "Clarity Call." This creates scarcity and ensures you only talk to high-quality leads.
   * The 1-on-1 Sales Call (The "Clarity Call"):
     * This 45-minute call is the entire sales process.
     * Script Focus:
       * Pain: "Tell me about your current role. What worries you about the next 5 years?" (Get them to repeat your premise back to you).
       * Dream: "If we were talking in 12 months, what would have to be true for you to be ecstatic with your career?"
       * The Gap: "What's stopping you from getting there on your own?"
       * The Pitch: "This is exactly what we solve. Our Accelerator is designed to bridge that gap. We use our Assessment Platform to find your baseline, our 'Agentic Framework' to build your new 'operating system,' and our Daily Coaching to ensure you never get stuck. We mold you into the AI Engineer you described."
       * The Close: "The investment for the program is $10,000. We have [X, Y] payment options. Based on everything you've told me, you are a perfect fit. Are you ready to do this?"
Phase 4: Delivery, Retention & Advocacy
Goal: To create "walking case studies" that fuel a referral engine and provide irrefutable proof for your marketing.
 * 1. Deliver a World-Class Experience: Your product must deliver. The daily coaching and assessment platform must be flawless. This is non-negotiable.
 * 2. Build a "Social Proof" Engine:
   * Community: Create a private Discord/Slack for all students.
   * Showcase: Mandate that students post their "Agentic Framework" projects on LinkedIn and tag your program. This is now your marketing.
   * Testimonials: Get video testimonials immediately upon a "big win" (e.g., they build their first agent, they get a promotion). Don't wait until the end.
 * 3. The B2B "Trojan Horse":
   * As your B2C graduates become successful, they become your entry point into the enterprise.
   * You can now approach their CTO (your secondary persona) and say: "We trained your new top performer, Alex. Imagine if your whole team had these 'agentic' skills. Let's talk about a pilot for your 10 senior devs."
   * This builds your B2B sales pipeline, which can sell higher-ticket cohort licenses ($50k - $100k+).

# AI Engineer Accelerator: Problem-Solution Fit, Funnels & MVP Strategy

## Part 1: Problem-Solution Fit Framework

### The Problem Stack (Validated vs. Assumed)

#### **Tier 1: Surface Problem (What prospects say)**

*“I need to learn AI/ML to stay relevant”*

**Validation Status**: ✅ **VALIDATED**

- LinkedIn shows 500%+ increase in “AI Engineer” job postings (2023-2025)
- 68% of tech workers report anxiety about AI replacing their role (Stack Overflow 2024)
- Search volume for “learn AI engineering” up 340% YoY

**Evidence Needed**: Survey 100 target personas with: *“What keeps you up at night about your career in the next 3 years?”*

-----

#### **Tier 2: Functional Problem (What they actually need)**

*“My specialized skills are becoming commoditized. I need to become more valuable.”*

**Validation Status**: ⚠️ **PARTIALLY VALIDATED**

**Your Hypothesis**:

- Traditional roles (backend dev, data scientist, ML engineer) are consolidating into “AI Engineer”
- Enterprises want “full-stack AI” generalists, not specialists
- Learning individual tools (LangChain, PyTorch) isn’t enough—you need a new “operating system” for thinking

**Critical Questions to Validate**:

1. **Are enterprises actually consolidating roles?** (Need: 10+ job descriptions showing this)
1. **Do hiring managers prefer generalists over specialists?** (Need: 20+ recruiter/CTO interviews)
1. **What’s the wage premium for “AI Engineers” vs. traditional roles?** (Need: salary data analysis)

**Red Flags to Watch**:

- If enterprises are creating *more* specialized AI roles (MLOps, AI Safety Engineer, Prompt Engineer), your premise collapses
- If “AI Engineer” becomes a junior role (vs. senior consolidator role), your $10K price point fails

-----

#### **Tier 3: Emotional Problem (What drives purchase decisions)**

*“I’m terrified of becoming obsolete. I want certainty that I’ll thrive, not just survive.”*

**Validation Status**: ✅ **VALIDATED** (this is universal fear in tech)

**Your Emotional Value Proposition**:

- **Fear → Confidence**: “Stop worrying about AI replacing you. Become the person who replaces jobs *with* AI.”
- **Confusion → Clarity**: “Stop drowning in tools and tutorials. Get a framework that tells you exactly what to learn and build.”
- **Isolation → Community**: “Stop learning alone. Join a cohort of ambitious professionals making the same leap.”

#### **The “Agentic Framework” (Your Proprietary IP)**

**Definition**: A mental model + skill stack for building AI systems that deliver business value, not just demos.

**The 5 Pillars** (draft—refine based on MVP):

1. **Problem Decomposition**

- Translate business problems into AI-solvable tasks
- Skills: Requirements gathering, system design, stakeholder communication

1. **Agentic Architecture**

- Design multi-agent systems (not just chatbots)
- Skills: LLM orchestration, tool use, memory management, error handling

1. **Full-Stack Integration**

- Deploy AI into production systems
- Skills: APIs, databases, frontend integration, monitoring

1. **Evaluation & Iteration**

- Measure AI system performance beyond “it works on my laptop”
- Skills: Metrics design, A/B testing, failure analysis

1. **Business Impact Translation**

- Communicate AI value in business terms
- Skills: ROI calculation, executive storytelling, roadmap planning

**Differentiation**:

- Not a “course” (passive learning)
- Not a “bootcamp” (race to build portfolio projects)
- It’s a **transformation program** (daily accountability + mental model shift)

#### **Stage 5: Application & Qualification (BOFU)**

**Objective**: Filter out 30% of applicants (only talk to qualified buyers)

**The Application Form** (7 questions):

1. **Full Name & Email** (basic)
1. **Current Role & Company** (persona validation)
1. **Annual Income** (can they afford $10K?)

- Options: <$50K / $50-75K / $75-100K / $100-150K / $150K+
- Auto-reject: <$50K (unless exceptional circumstances)

1. **Why do you want to become an AI Engineer?** (open text)

- Looking for: Clarity of motivation + career urgency

1. **What’s your biggest obstacle to becoming an AI Engineer?** (open text)

- Looking for: Self-awareness + realistic obstacles

1. **Have you tried learning AI before? What happened?** (open text)

- Looking for: Past behavior = future behavior. Need evidence of commitment.

1. **On a scale of 1-10, how ready are you to invest $10,000 in your career transformation?**

- Auto-reject: 1-7
- Manual review: 8-10

**Application Review Process**:

- Review applications within 24 hours
- Score: 0-10 (based on income + motivation + readiness)
- Send calendar link to 8+ scores only
- Rejection email (for <8): “Thank you for applying. Based on your responses, I don’t think the program is the right fit at this time. I’d recommend [free resource] as a starting point.”

#### **Stage 6: The Sales Call (BOFU)**

**Objective**: Close 25-33% of qualified calls

**The 45-Minute “Career Clarity Call” Script**:

**Phase 1: Rapport & Discovery** (10 min)

- “Thanks for applying. I reviewed your form. Tell me more about [something from their application].”
- Let them talk. Listen for:
  - Career pain points
  - Learning history (what they’ve tried)
  - Timeline urgency (promotion coming up? Layoff fear?)
  - Budget reality (can they actually afford this?)

**Phase 2: Pain Amplification** (10 min)

- “So it sounds like you’re worried about [X]. Can you help me understand what happens if you don’t solve this in the next year?”
- Get them to articulate the cost of inaction
- “And you mentioned you’ve tried [learning method]. Why didn’t that work?”
- Position past failures as validation of need for program

**Phase 3: Solution Presentation** (15 min)

- “Based on what you’ve told me, I think you’re a great fit for the Accelerator. Here’s how it works…”
- Walk through the 3 components (Assessment, Framework, Coaching)
- Tailor to their specific gaps: “You mentioned [X] is your weakness. Here’s exactly how we address that…”
- Share case study of someone similar to them

**Phase 4: Investment & Close** (10 min)

- “The investment for the program is $10,000. We offer two payment options:”
  - Option 1: $10,000 upfront
  - Option 2: $3,500 down + $3,500 at 30 days + $3,500 at 60 days
- Silence. Let them process.
- Handle objections:
  - **“I need to think about it”** → “What specifically do you need to think about? Let’s talk through it now.”
  - **“I need to talk to my spouse”** → “Of course. What questions do you think they’ll have? Let’s make sure you’re prepared to explain the value.”
  - **“That’s a lot of money”** → “It is. And what’s it worth to you to never worry about your career again?”
- Close: “Are you ready to move forward?”

**Call Tech Stack**:

- Platform: Zoom (record for training purposes)
- CRM: HubSpot or [Close.io](http://Close.io) (track leads, follow-ups)
- Payment: Stripe (send payment link during call)

## Part 3: MVP Strategy (Months 1-6)

### MVP Philosophy

**Goal**: Validate problem-solution fit with 10-15 customers before scaling marketing.

**Anti-Pattern to Avoid**: Building the full platform before proving anyone will pay.

**The Right Sequence**:

1. ✅ Sell the program (prove demand)
1. ✅ Deliver manually (prove outcomes)
1. ✅ Build the platform (scale delivery)

-----

### MVP Phase 1: Pre-Launch Validation (Month 1-2)

**Objective**: Get 3-5 people to give you $5,000 (50% discount) for a “pilot cohort”

**Tactics**:

**Week 1-2: Create Minimum Viable Content**

- Record 3 “Provocation” videos (The Great Consolidation)
- Create 1 lead magnet (simple assessment using Google Forms)
- Write 3-email nurture sequence
- Create basic landing page (Carrd or Webflow)

**Week 3-4: Guerrilla Marketing**

- Post videos on YouTube + LinkedIn
- Reach out to 50 people in your network:
  - “Hey [Name], I’m launching a program to help [persona] become AI Engineers. You came to mind. Are you interested or know anyone who would be?”
- Goal: Get 100 emails + book 10 “pilot discovery calls”

**Week 5-6: Pilot Sales Calls**

- No webinar yet (1-on-1 pitches only)
- Offer: “I’m running a pilot cohort. Normally $10K, but you’ll get it for $5K as an early adopter. Start date: [4 weeks from now].”
- Close 3-5 people

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ 3+ people pay $5K (proves demand)
- ✅ You hear the same pain points in every call (confirms persona)
- ❌ If you can’t get 3 people to pay, your premise may be wrong—iterate

-----

### MVP Phase 2: Pilot Cohort Delivery (Month 3-5)

**Objective**: Deliver a life-changing experience for 3-5 people, manually

**What You Deliver** (90 days):

**Week 1-2: Assessment & Onboarding**

- Manual 1-on-1 assessment interview (1 hour each)
- Baseline skills audit: “Where are you now?”
- Create personalized learning path (in a Google Doc)
- Onboard to private Slack channel

**Week 3-8: Framework + Projects (The Core)**

- Weekly group coaching call (90 min)
  - 30 min: Teach 1 pillar of Agentic Framework
  - 60 min: Project work session (students work, you coach)
- Daily async support in Slack
- Students build 3 projects (one every 2 weeks):

1. Project 1: Simple AI agent (tool use + LLM)
1. Project 2: Full-stack AI app (API + frontend)
1. Project 3: Business-value project (solve a real problem)

**Week 9-12: Career Positioning**

- Resume/LinkedIn overhaul (position as “AI Engineer”)
- Interview prep (technical + behavioral)
- Portfolio refinement (showcase projects)
- Job search strategy or internal promotion pitch

**What You DON’T Build Yet**:

- ❌ Automated assessment platform (do it manually on Zoom)
- ❌ Course platform (use Notion + Loom videos)
- ❌ Community platform (just use Slack)

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ 80%+ students complete the program
- ✅ 80%+ students ship all 3 projects
- ✅ 100% students say “I’d recommend this” (NPS 9-10)
- ✅ 3+ students agree to video testimonials

-----

### MVP Phase 3: Optimization & Content (Month 6)

**Objective**: Create the marketing content you need to scale

**Deliverables**:

1. **3 Case Study Videos** (5-10 min each)

- Interview pilot students
- Before/After story
- Show their projects
- Use these in webinar + email sequence

1. **Refine the “Agentic Framework”**

- Based on what actually worked in pilot
- Create a visual diagram (hire designer on Fiverr)
- Turn into a 15-page PDF lead magnet

1. **First Webinar Recording**

- Do a “live” webinar for pilot grads + their networks
- Record it
- Use recording as evergreen funnel asset

1. **Build V1 of Assessment Platform**

- Automate the skills audit (Typeform + scoring logic)
- This becomes your lead magnet going forward

**Investment at This Stage**:

- ~$5K (videographer, designer, tools)
- 100-150 hours of your time

-----

### MVP Phase 4: Scale Readiness (Month 7+)

**Decision Point**: Do you have product-market fit?

**Checklist**:

- ✅ 80%+ pilot completion rate
- ✅ 80%+ student success rate (meaningful outcome)
- ✅ 100% would recommend (NPS 9-10)
- ✅ Students achieved outcomes in <120 days
- ✅ You can articulate what “success” looks like
- ✅ You have 3+ video testimonials
- ✅ You can deliver the program without burning out

**If YES → Scale**:

- Start the full funnel (YouTube → Webinar → Sales)
- Set revenue goal: $30K/month (3 sales)
- Invest in ads (once organic funnel works)

**If NO → Iterate**:

- What went wrong? (Curriculum? Coaching? Persona?)
- Offer pilot grads a refund or free continuation
- Go back to Phase 1 with new insights
